Live in four steps

  • Start in the sandbox

    Get API keys in a few clicks, supply test USDC and open a test credit line.

  • Build it in

    One API for yield and credit, under your brand, with your own fee on each draw, from 0% to 4%. You go live after a short onboarding check.

  • Your users earn or borrow

    USDC they supply earns the interest borrowers pay. Bitcoin they pledge moves into its own vault, and USDC settles in about 30 minutes, when liquidity is available.

  • Withdraw or repay

    USDC that isn't lent out leaves the variable pool when they choose, and credit lines repay any time within 12 months.

Questions

Where does the yield come from?

Interest paid by borrowers on the USDC they draw, not token emissions. Every credit line is over-collateralized by Bitcoin held in its own vault.

Who runs KYC?

You do, under your own policies. Users never sign up with Surge, and every integrator goes through Surge's onboarding check before launch.

Do we need a lending license?

Surge is software and isn't the lender: the USDC comes from independent lenders. Whether you need a license depends on your markets and how you offer credit, so we walk your counsel through the structure before you launch.

Can we read rates and positions programmatically?

Yes. Every vault, pool and rate is live on Surge Markets, and the same market data is served by a public API.

Which networks?

USDC from other networks can be supplied too, never wrapped. The details are in Surge Docs.
