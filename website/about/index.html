Leadership

Talk to the founders

  • Founder and CEO

    Yash Belavadi

    Yash founded Surge and leads the company. A computer scientist by training, he was Chief Product Officer at Frontier Wallet before Surge, and he writes Surge's explainers on how Bitcoin-backed credit works.

  • Co-founder and CTO

    Michael Borglin

    Michael has built Bitcoin products since 2018. He co-founded Lightning Ramp, which connected exchanges to the Lightning Network, joined Casa to build the Casa Lightning Node, and has worked in custody operations at Coinbase.

  • Co-founder and CSO

    Punith B M

    Punith has spent more than ten years in software engineering, working on wallets, multi-party computation, key management and security. He writes about how Surge keeps collateral secure.
