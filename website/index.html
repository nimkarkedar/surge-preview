How a credit line runs in your app

  1. You integrate

    Add the SDK. Point your coding agent at the docs; one team shipped a working flow in an afternoon [VERIFY].

  2. Your user locks BTC

    In a Taproot vault on Bitcoin, theirs alone. Never wrapped, never pooled.

  3. They draw dollars

    USDC arrives in about 30 minutes, inside your app.

  4. They repay

    Any time, and the same coins come back. If Surge ever disappears, they can still exit alone.

Built on Bitcoin's terms

Audited by Rearden Code (Bitcoin layer) and CredShields (smart contracts). Every finding is public.

  • No wrapping

    BTC stays native on Bitcoin. No bridge, no IOU token.

  • Its own vault

    Every credit line has its own vault. The same coins go in and come out.

  • A way out, always

    If Surge disappeared tomorrow, users could still recover their Bitcoin. Bitcoin script enforces it.
