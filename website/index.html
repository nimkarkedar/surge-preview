Questions

What is Bitcoin-backed credit?

A credit line secured by Bitcoin. The borrower pledges BTC as collateral and draws dollars (USDC) against it, without selling the Bitcoin.

How do platforms integrate Surge?

Through one API, the Surge API. Get API keys and open a test credit line in the sandbox in a few clicks.

How do platforms earn?

Through a draw fee you set, from 0% to 4% of each draw, flat or tiered by size. Your customer sees it before signing, and it's paid to you in USDC at the draw.

Does Surge take custody of my customers' Bitcoin?

No. Each credit line has its own vault on the Bitcoin network, and no one, Surge included, can move the Bitcoin alone. It can leave the vault only three ways: repayment, liquidation or your customer's own recovery.

How fast do funds arrive?

About 30 minutes after the collateral is confirmed, when liquidity is available.

What if Surge stops operating?

Customers can still recover their Bitcoin on their own, after a waiting period, with open-source tools. The Bitcoin network enforces it, not a contract with us.
