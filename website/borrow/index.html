You stay in control

What if Bitcoin's price falls?

Your credit line opens at no more than half your Bitcoin's value, so there's room for the price to move, and the calculator above shows the price at which your line would close. You can repay or add Bitcoin at any time. If a line does close, enough Bitcoin is sold to repay what you owe plus a penalty, and the rest comes back to you.

Can I repay early?

Yes. Repay any time within the 12-month term, and the same Bitcoin is returned to you.

Where is my Bitcoin?

In a dedicated vault on the Bitcoin network, created for your credit line only. You can check it at any time on Surge Markets ↗ (opens another site)
