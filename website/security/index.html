Four guarantees, each verifiable

Every credit line has its own vault on the Bitcoin network, every vault is public and live, and two independent security firms, Rearden Code and CredShields, reviewed Surge end to end.

  • Never lent out

    Collateral is never lent out or reused.

    Verify on Surge Markets ↗ (opens another site)

  • Segregated vaults

    One vault per credit line. The same Bitcoin in and out.

    Verify on Surge Markets ↗ (opens another site)

  • Recoverable without Surge

    Clients can always recover their Bitcoin alone, after a waiting period.

    Read the recovery path ↗ (opens another site)

  • No one acts alone

    No one, Surge included, can move collateral alone, and it can leave its vault only by repayment, liquidation or the client's own recovery.

Questions

Is Surge secure?

Security is built into every credit line. Each one has its own vault on the Bitcoin network, no one, Surge included, can move the Bitcoin alone, clients can always recover it without Surge, and two independent audits are published in full.

Is Surge audited?

Yes, twice, by Rearden Code and CredShields, two independent security firms. Both reports are public, every finding included.

Does Surge lend out customers' Bitcoin?

No. Each credit line's Bitcoin sits in its own vault and is never lent out or reused.

Can Surge or anyone else take the collateral?

No. Nobody can move it alone, Surge included, and a vault allows only three outcomes: repayment, liquidation or the client's own recovery.
